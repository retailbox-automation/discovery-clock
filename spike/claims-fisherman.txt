Deputies responded to a report of a possible drowning in the marina.
The area was behind a locked gate.
Sgt. Leven was the first deputy on scene.
A man had fallen out of his wheelchair into the marina.
The man was clinging to a pipe to keep his head above water.
Sgt. Leven jumped into the water with a buoy.
The man was floated to safety.
The man was evaluated by fire rescue and declined further medical attention.
A passing boater pulled the man out of the water before deputies arrived.
The man told deputies he had been drinking.
